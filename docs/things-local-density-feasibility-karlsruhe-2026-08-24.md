# Things Concierge Alpha — Karlsruhe Local-Density Feasibility

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Owner:** Worker 4  
**Mode:** Public, read-only desk research; no business or device owner was contacted.  
**Decision:** PASS_KARLSRUHE_PRE_OUTREACH_DENSITY

## Question

Can the unchanged 30-day concierge-alpha design plausibly fill five qualified professional buyer seats and 25 owner intakes inside one radius of no more than 15 km around central Karlsruhe, without changing the product or starting outreach?

This screen applies the CEO-approved gate from the Hambrücken/Bruchsal review:

- at least ten distinct professional prospects inside the radius;
- at least five with first-party public evidence of buying, reselling, or refurbishing smartphones;
- no more than two branches controlled by one corporate purchasing policy;
- more than 25,000 residents;
- practical daytime handover access;
- CEO approval of area and operator before any outreach.

## Geographic and owner-side fit

Karlsruhe's official city statistics report about 299,400 residents in the current city baseline used by the municipal population forecast. This exceeds the 25,000-resident floor by roughly twelve times before including any surrounding municipality.

Twenty-five owner intakes equal approximately 0.0084% of 299,400 residents. This makes the quota numerically plausible, but it is not a response-rate forecast. The earlier national unused-phone evidence remains category context only.

All ten screened businesses expose a Karlsruhe address in postal-code areas 76131, 76133, 76137 or 76185. The pool is therefore concentrated well inside a 15 km central-Karlsruhe screen. Several are in the Kaiserstraße/Kaiserallee, Bahnhofplatz or inner-city corridor; SmartPunkt's local page explicitly documents tram/stadtbahn access from Marktplatz and Europaplatz. Daytime local handover access passes as a desk-research condition only; safety and participant preference remain untested.

## Funnel sufficiency

The alpha needs five qualified buyers, not merely five shops.

- Five seats from ten visible prospects require 50.0% prospect-to-qualified conversion.
- Five seats from the eight purchase-evidenced prospects require 62.5%.
- The six first-party-evidenced prospects alone provide one-candidate redundancy over the five-seat target.
- Compared with Hambrücken/Bruchsal's seven prospects and 71.4% conversion requirement, Karlsruhe improves the visible top-of-funnel count by 42.9% and adds four first-party-evidenced purchase/refurbishment surfaces (six versus two).

This is the minimum approved location gate, not strong demand evidence. A 50% prospect conversion remains ambitious, and a 33% planning assumption would require fifteen prospects. Karlsruhe is suitable for a capped outreach experiment only because it clears every fixed pre-outreach threshold; it does not prove that five buyers can be recruited.

## Decision

**PASS_KARLSRUHE_PRE_OUTREACH_DENSITY**

- Owner-side population floor: PASS.
- Distinct professional prospect count: PASS at 10.
- First-party purchase/resale/refurbishment count: PASS at 6.
- Corporate concentration: PASS; branches of the same organization are collapsed.
- Practical daytime access: PASS for desk-research purposes.
- Things-specific buyer pain, recurring intent, availability and willingness to pay: UNKNOWN.
- Hosted non-privileged auth/RLS evidence: still a separate prerequisite for execution.
- CEO authorization of area and accountable operator: still required before outreach.

The result permits Karlsruhe to become the preferred candidate location after the hosted security gate. It does not authorize outreach, participant-data collection, payments, publication or product changes.

## Reversible next step after prerequisites

After the hosted non-privileged security test is green, the CEO may authorize one Karlsruhe operator to approach only the prioritized independent first-party-evidenced pool, capped at ten business prospects. Stop and return HOLD if fewer than three real buyer intents emerge from the first five completed buyer conversations or if fraud/ownership controls cannot be accepted.

The existing 30-day commercial gates remain unchanged: five qualified buyers, 25 owners, at least three real buyer intents, ten eligible devices, three candidate matches, one bilateral price-confirmed handover, two credible price acceptances, no unresolved serious incident, and the stricter direct-labor economics thresholds.

## Limitations

Search visibility can omit small or newly opened businesses. A public purchase service does not prove local purchasing authority, recurring sourcing pain, capacity, budget, willingness to join Things, or willingness to pay EUR 29/month or EUR 5/match. Some public pages may change after the review date. Those facts require separately authorized participant contact. This assessment therefore passes only the fixed location-density gate and makes no revenue forecast.
