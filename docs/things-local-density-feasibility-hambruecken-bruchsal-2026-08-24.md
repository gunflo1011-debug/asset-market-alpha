# Things Concierge Alpha — Hambrücken/Bruchsal Local-Density Feasibility

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Owner:** Worker 4  
**Mode:** Public, read-only desk research; no business or owner was contacted.  
**Decision:** NO_GO_HAMBRUECKEN_15KM_BUYER_FUNNEL

## Question

Can the existing 30-day concierge-alpha design plausibly fill five qualified professional buyer seats and 25 owner intakes inside one radius of no more than 15 km around Hambrücken without changing the product or starting outreach?

## Geographic fit

Bruchsal is approximately 7.6 km straight-line and 10.4 km by road from Hambrücken, so its business center fits inside a 15 km Hambrücken radius. The corridor also contains Forst and reaches parts or all of several neighboring municipalities.

Public population evidence is sufficient for a supply-side feasibility judgment:

- Bruchsal reports 47,382 residents as of 2025-12-31.
- Hambrücken's official site states that it has exceeded 5,000 residents.
- Forst reports approximately 8,100 residents.
- These three places alone represent more than 60,000 residents before counting Waghäusel, Graben-Neudorf, Karlsdorf-Neuthard, Ubstadt-Weiher, or other nearby municipalities.

Twenty-five completed owner intakes equal less than 0.05% of that conservative three-place population base. Population is not conversion evidence, but the numerical owner quota is not the primary density constraint. The prior national unused-phone evidence remains category context only and is not projected directly onto this local population.

Result:

- 7 plausible professional prospects are publicly visible.
- Only 2 have direct first-party evidence of local handset purchase service.
- A third has secondary directory evidence of purchase service.
- The remaining 4 are relevant repair/retail operators but cannot be counted as qualified buyers without contact.
- Zero has evidenced willingness to submit recurring private-inventory intents or pay EUR 29/month or EUR 5/match.

## Funnel sufficiency test

The alpha needs five qualified buyers, not merely five shops.

With seven plausible prospects:

- filling five seats requires 71.4% prospect-to-qualified conversion;
- if only the three purchase-evidenced prospects are treated as high-fit, the target is mathematically impossible before discovering additional candidates;
- a conservative 50% prospect-to-qualified planning assumption requires at least ten plausible prospects;
- a 33% planning assumption requires at least fifteen.

The present public pool therefore does not provide enough acquisition redundancy. One refusal, central-chain purchasing restriction, lack of recurring sourcing pain, or pricing rejection would materially jeopardize the test.

This is a feasibility assessment, not a claim that the businesses would decline. No inference about any named business's private purchasing policy is made.

## Decision

**NO_GO_HAMBRUECKEN_15KM_BUYER_FUNNEL**

- Owner-side population density: PASS for a later acquisition test, while actual response rate remains unknown.
- Professional-buyer prospect count: FAIL for a five-seat test with reasonable funnel redundancy.
- Explicit local purchasing evidence: FAIL; only two first-party and one secondary-evidenced prospect.
- Willingness to pay and recurring intent: UNKNOWN.
- Safe next action under current authorization: stop before outreach.

Do not launch the five-buyer/25-owner alpha in the Hambrücken-centered 15 km radius based on the current desk evidence.

## Reversible next decision

The CEO may later choose exactly one of these, after the hosted security gate is green:

1. **Relocate without changing the test:** conduct a new public-density screen around a denser center such as Karlsruhe and require at least ten plausible prospects, including five with explicit purchase/refurbishment activity, before any outreach.
2. **Change the experimental design:** reduce the buyer-seat target or broaden the radius, which requires a new CEO decision because it changes the accepted 30-day contract.
3. **Kill the Things concierge test:** if no compliant local area can produce at least ten plausible prospects and five explicit purchase/refurbishment prospects.

No outreach-area decision is needed while the hosted Supabase gate remains blocked.

## Pre-outreach location gate

A candidate area may pass to outreach only if all are true:

- at least ten distinct professional prospects inside the approved radius;
- at least five have first-party public evidence of buying, reselling, or refurbishing smartphones;
- no more than two are branches controlled by the same corporate purchasing policy;
- owner population exceeds 25,000;
- local daytime handover locations and public transport/road access are practical;
- the CEO has explicitly approved the area and operator.

## Limitations

Search visibility can omit small or newly opened businesses. Public repair/retail activity does not prove local acquisition authority, recurring purchasing pain, budget, availability, or price acceptance. Those facts require authorized participant contact. Consequently, this review is intentionally fail-closed and does not convert public listings into demand evidence.
